## Context

**The bars by leg, the charts by stage.** The cockpit folds the job's fifteen steps into four legs, each a bar (ADR-361, ADR-394): deduplication, the census to content redundancy; relevance, seed extraction to the relevance floor; clustering, the clustering and the relevance report; generation, the arrangement and the generation. Its charts are one for each stage, eight in two rows beneath the steps and the output (ADR-382 §1). The two groupings do not line up: stage 5's chart draws the steps of two legs, and nothing on screen links a bar to its chart.

**A stage gone through finished.** Under ADR-418 §3 a stage whose steps go through already finished, their run completed in an earlier invocation, freezes holding one sample of its kinds, at second 0, and no `done`; ADR-422 draws that sample as a marker, and ADR-425 centres the axes on it. Where none of the stage's steps said a counter in the invocation, `done` has no point (ADR-382 §5), and a stage of no kind draws nothing. Nothing on the chart says the work was done in an earlier invocation, so it reads as a stage that took no time.

**The tabs.** The tabs pane beneath the charts holds the Report tab, `LiveReport` (ADR-362 §8, ADR-417 §4), and the Inclusions tab, `InclusionsTab` (ADR-345 §2). The Report tab's list of the twelve analysis reports is the sidebar's Reports section's list, each opening its report in the Reports window (ADR-414). Its other parts are the status line and its stale mark, the display and what was opened, the gates with their keys, the refusals, the faults by type, the closing line, the next value and the files. The Inclusions tab edits the folders not walked and the folders not relevant, the same profile the sidebar's Folders section points the window at.

**What the jar knows of each step.** `JobSampler` holds each step's last progress line, which a counter, `N of T (P%)` or `N so far`, gives `done` (ADR-382 §5); the census's own line gives the entries walked (ADR-412 §2). The steps and the kinds of verdict each records, in `VerdictKind`'s order (`JobSampler.OUTCOME_STEPS`): byte-level reduction `broken`, `duplicate-of`, `superseded-by`, `out-of-scope` and `code`; extraction `extraction-failed` and `degenerate-output`; content redundancy `redundant-with`; the relevance floor `below-threshold`; the eleven others none, `passed` recorded by no step. The run each step records its completion under is `InvocationReport.STEPS_OF_A_STAGE`'s: byte-level reduction, extraction, content census and the arrangement and generation each their own; the redundancy signatures and content redundancy the content-redundancy run; seed extraction and the seed-corpus comparison the seed-measurement run; embedding scoring, relevance scoring and the clustering the embedding-scoring run. The relevance floor, which records its verdicts under the embedding-scoring run, and the relevance report record no completion (ADR-118); the census records none, working under a walk. The runs reading made as the invocation starts lists, of every run the working directory holds, the steps its completion is recorded for (`Runs.finishedSteps`, ADR-269 §5).

**The room.** At the window's opening size, 1,500 by 960, the column beside the 420 px sidebar is 1,040 px wide (ADR-382, Context); a leg's content, set in by its pane, is narrower. Three charts of 320 px and two spaces of the stylesheet's 8 px stand in 976 px, and four in 1,304 px.

## Decision

### 1. The main column

**Top to bottom**: the header, the outcome line, the card of the actions and what the next invocation wants, then `output-and-legs`, a `SplitPane` up and down filling the rest of the column's height.

| Part | Id | What it holds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The split | `output-and-legs` | a `SplitPane`, vertical, of two items and one divider the operator drags |
| Its first item | `output-and-ending` | ADR-417's split, unchanged within: `output` above, the ending panel beneath it where it has one |
| Its second item | `legs-area` | a `ScrollPane` fitted to its width, scrolling up and down on its own, holding `steps`, the four legs (ADR-361 §3) |

- **The output spans the column**: nothing stands beside `output-and-ending`, which is as wide as the split. The steps stand below it, in `legs-area`, and no longer beside it; `steps-and-output` and `steps-and-output-grip` are gone.
- **The divider** is the split's own: dragged down, the output and its ending panel grow by what the legs area loses, and up the other way. Where it stands is kept while the window is open, as ADR-417 §1 keeps a size the operator drags to, and written nowhere. `sidebar-grip` stays as ADR-417 §1 has it.
- **The output opens 300 px high**: as the window opens, `output-and-ending` stands 300 px high and `legs-area` takes the rest.
- **The column does not scroll as a whole**: no `ScrollPane` holds `output-and-legs`, so the legs area, not the window, scrolls when the legs open are taller than it.

### 2. A chart for each step, beneath its leg's bars

**Each leg's pane holds, beneath its steps' rows, `leg-charts-<leg>`, a pane of its steps' charts, one `step-chart-<step>` for each of its steps, in step order**: the fifteen `LineChart`s of the job's steps, each of style class `progress-chart`, a step being in exactly one leg (ADR-361 §1). They fold and open with their leg (§7).

- **A grid**: the charts of a leg stand in as many columns as fit at 320 px each and the stylesheet's space between them, at least one; each column an equal share of the pane's width; the charts fill the columns in step order, a row at a time, a last row of fewer leaving the rest of its width empty. At the window's opening size that is three columns: deduplication's six charts in two rows of three, relevance's five in a row of three and one of two, clustering's and generation's two in one row each.
- **Each chart is at least 320 px wide and 240 px high.** Nothing scrolls sideways: a narrower leg holds fewer columns.
- **Its title is its step's name as `Legs.ALL` writes it**, `byte-level-reduction`, the name its bar's row shows, wrapping where the chart is too narrow; then *, finished at `HH:mm`* once frozen (§6).
- **Its axes** are ADR-382 §2's, a step's: the time axis *Elapsed*; the value axis *Done and verdicts (count)* on a step that records a kind of verdict, byte-level reduction, extraction, content redundancy and the relevance floor, and *Done (count)* on the others. Each is set from the chart's own samples as ADR-425 sets a stage's.
- **A legend** for two lines or more, and none for one, as ADR-365 §1 has it.

`stage-charts`, its two rows and the eight `stage-chart-<stage>` are gone.

### 3. What a step's chart plots

**First `done`, the step's own count, then one line for each kind of verdict the step records, in `VerdictKind`'s order, named as `CONTEXT.md` writes the kind**:

| Step | Lines |
| --- | --- |
| `census` | `done` |
| `byte-level-reduction` | `done`, `broken`, `duplicate-of`, `superseded-by`, `out-of-scope`, `code` |
| `extraction` | `done`, `extraction-failed`, `degenerate-output` |
| `content-census`, `redundancy-signature` | `done` |
| `content-redundancy` | `done`, `redundant-with` |
| `seed-extraction`, `seed-corpus-comparison`, `embedding-scoring`, `relevance-scoring` | `done` |
| `relevance-floor` | `done`, `below-threshold` |
| `clustering`, `relevance-report`, `arrangement`, `generation` | `done` |

- **`done`** at a sample is the count done of the counter the step itself said last in this invocation, `N` of *`N of T (P%)`* or *`N so far`*; for the census, the entries walked its own line said last of the walk walked (ADR-412 §2, ADR-418 §4). Where the step has said none in this invocation, `done` has no point at that sample.
- **A kind's value** at a sample is the count of that kind standing under the step's run: what the runs reading at the invocation's start counted of it under that run, 0 where that reading failed or does not list it, plus the verdicts of that kind recorded in this invocation, less those discarded (ADR-382 §5, ADR-418 §2). A kind's line is drawn from the chart's first sample, at 0 where none stands.
- **A chart draws six lines at most**, byte-level reduction's.

### 4. A step's chart is kept in a file of its own, written by the jar

**`vespera run`'s job writes, at the top of the working directory beside `report.json`, one file for each of the fifteen steps, `chart-step-<step>.json`**, `<step>` as `Legs.ALL` and the report write it: `chart-step-census.json` to `chart-step-generation.json`. It writes them as ADR-382 §4 and ADR-418 write a stage's, whole, through a temporary file moved over it, a file that cannot be written warned of once in the invocation and failing nothing. **It writes no `chart-stage-<stage>.json`, and deletes none**: a stage file an earlier version wrote stays where it lies, and nothing reads it. `vespera label` and `vespera publish` write none.

```json
{
  "format": 1,
  "step": "extraction",
  "key": { "extraction": "<run id>" },
  "finishedAt": "2026-11-20T10:35:05Z",
  "finishedEarlier": false,
  "lines": [
    { "name": "done", "seconds": [0, 5, 10], "values": [120, 260, 400] },
    { "name": "extraction-failed", "seconds": [0, 5, 10], "values": [0, 3, 4] },
    { "name": "degenerate-output", "seconds": [0, 5, 10], "values": [0, 1, 1] }
  ]
}
```

- `format`: 1, raised by a change that breaks a reader; a reader draws a file of another format as no file.
- `step`: the step.
- `key`: what the chart is kept under: the census's the walk, `{"walk": "<walk id>"}`, as ADR-418 §4 moves it from the walk walked to the walk handed; every other step's the run it works under, by its run stage: `byte-level-reduction`, `extraction`, `content-census`, `arrangement` or `generation` for the step of that name; `content-redundancy` for the redundancy signatures and content redundancy; `seed-measurement` for seed extraction and the seed-corpus comparison; `embedding-scoring` for embedding scoring, relevance scoring, the relevance floor, the clustering and the relevance report. **A step's key is written at its chart's first sample**, not as the invocation reaches its run: a step whose run an earlier step reached keeps the empty file until it runs itself.
- `finishedAt`: the instant the step finished in this invocation, to the second in UTC, once its chart is frozen (§6); `null` until then, and for a step finished in an earlier invocation.
- `finishedEarlier`: `true` for a step finished in an earlier invocation (§5), else `false`.
- `lines`: §3's lines in §3's order, each with its own `seconds` and `values`, of one length, oldest first; none for a step finished in an earlier invocation.

**At the job's first reading each of the fifteen is written empty**, as ADR-418 §1 writes a stage's, whatever it held:

```json
{ "format": 1, "step": "extraction", "key": {}, "finishedAt": null, "finishedEarlier": false, "lines": [] }
```

**A step's chart takes its samples as ADR-382 §5 has a stage's take them, of the step alone**: at each reading at which the step stands `running`; at the reading at which it freezes; and at the job's last reading where it ran in this invocation. Its seconds count from its first sample in this invocation, and 256 samples at most are kept, thinned as ADR-382 §5 thins them. **The census's chart is ADR-418 §4's chart of stage 0**, written as it walks, kept under the walk it hands the job, and written empty where the census ends with no walk handed.

**The window reads the fifteen files** when ADR-382 §4 has it read the stages', as it opens, whenever another working directory is named and at each reading of the report, through `StepChartFile`, a class of its own built on its `JsonReader` in place of `StageChartFile`; and **it reads no `chart-stage-<stage>.json`**. A file not there, not readable, not that JSON, of another `format`, or naming another step, is drawn as no file, and nothing is said. The window draws what each file holds and decides none of it.

### 5. What a chart shows in each of its states

The window tells a chart's state from its file alone:

| State | The file | The chart |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Not started** | not there, drawn as no file (§4), or `key` empty | its title, its axes as ADR-425 §4 draws an axis of no number, no point, no line, no legend, and no text in its plot |
| **Finished in an earlier invocation** | `finishedEarlier` `true` | its title, its step's name alone; its axes as ADR-425 §4 draws them; no point, no line, no legend; and, centred in its plot, *finished in an earlier invocation* |
| **Ran, and reported no count** | `key` given, `finishedEarlier` `false`, and `done` holding no point | centred in its plot, *no count reported*; its kinds' lines drawn as the file holds them, frozen or not as `finishedAt` says |
| **Ran, with a count** | `key` given and `done` holding a point | its lines; no text in its plot |

- **The words** are `step-chart-words-<step>`, one text for each chart, laid over its plot, centred on the plot area, wrapping within it, taking no pointer, drawn in the stylesheet's colour for a chart's title at AA in both themes (ADR-374 §1). Where a chart is in neither of the two states that carry words, it shows none: the text is empty, or not shown.
- **The jar says a step finished in an earlier invocation**: at the first reading at which the step stands `finished`, where the runs reading made as the invocation started lists the step finished under the run this invocation reached for it, the chart freezes holding no sample, `finishedAt` `null`, `finishedEarlier` `true` and no line, and is not written again in the invocation. Its `finishedAt` is `null` because the instant it first finished is recorded nowhere: the database holds no time (ADR-382, Context). The census, the relevance floor and the relevance report record no completion under a run, so none of them is ever one; they decide again on every invocation (ADR-118), and the census walks on every one.
- **A step that went through finished where the runs reading failed** is drawn as one that ran: it freezes as §6 has it, holding one sample of its kinds and no `done`, so *no count reported* (Known limits).

### 6. A finished step's chart is frozen

**A step's chart freezes at the first reading at which the step stands `finished`, after it takes that reading's sample**, as ADR-382 §6 freezes a stage's: its `finishedAt` is the step's end in this invocation, and it is not written again in the invocation that froze it (ADR-418 §2). A step stopped at a gate, failed or stopped has not finished. **The window draws it frozen where `finishedAt` is given**, as ADR-382 §6 and ADR-422 draw a stage's: *, finished at `HH:mm`* in its title, in the machine's zone; the style class `frozen`; its lines and its legend's swatches at opacity 0.85, its lines 1.5 px wide; no sample marked `latest-sample`; and the one sample of a line holding one alone marked `only-sample`, dimmed as its line.

### 7. The legs open as they do now

**Which legs are open is ADR-361's, unchanged**: at each reading of the report a leg is open where it runs or failed or holds the first step stopped at a gate (§5), and a leg the operator opens or folds stays as they left it for as long as the window is open, across every later reading (§6). A leg's charts stand inside its pane, so a folded leg shows its bar and no chart, and an open one its rows and its charts.

### 8. The tabs pane goes; the Report tab's contents go to the header and the ending panel

**The cockpit's window holds no `TabPane`.** `live-report` and `inclusions` are gone, with `LiveReport` and `InclusionsTab`.

- **The status line and its stale mark stand in the header**, `report-status` and `report-stale`, under `database-size`, in the column `where`, `child` and `database-size` stand in. They are shown, and laid out, while the window watches an invocation that has not ended, its own child or a foreign holder's (ADR-362 §8), and neither is shown otherwise: before any invocation is watched, and once the watched one has ended, when the ending panel says how. What the status line says and when the mark shows are ADR-362 §8's: *waiting for this invocation's report* until the watched invocation's report is read, then its command, its state, its outcome, the time it was written and its interval; *stale* where it says it runs and was written more than three of its intervals before.
- **Once the invocation has ended, the ending panel says all the tab said of it but the display and the reports**, from the report of the ending it shows, the window's own child's own report or the earlier invocation's (ADR-417 §2): the refusals and the closing line, in `ending-line`, and the faults by type, in `ending-faults`, as ADR-417 §3 has them; and, beneath `ending-faults`, three parts of its own, each a selectable text or texts, shown and laid out only where it holds something:

  | Part | Id | What it says |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | The gates | `ending-gates` | for each gate, the step stopped at it, the profile key whose absence stopped it, and the analysis reports that key is read off, by name |
  | The next value | `ending-next` | the report's `next`, the value the next invocation wants |
  | The files | `ending-files` | each file the report names written, its role and its path; the deliverable's index, where the report's places name a deliverable root and its index is there, as a link handed to the system, as the tab handed it |

  An ending whose child wrote no report of its own shows none of the three, its last line of output standing in `ending-line` (ADR-417 §3). Nothing of the three shows while an invocation runs, the panel showing nothing then (ADR-417 §2).
- **Open report goes**: the tab it selected is gone, and what the tab drew of the ending's report is in the panel itself. `ending-open-report` is not drawn; Open profile and Copy details stay, Copy details copying the three parts beneath the others, top to bottom, a line each.
- **The list of analysis reports is not drawn anywhere but in the sidebar's Reports section**, which opens each in the Reports window (ADR-414). The display and what was opened, which the tab drew in `report-display`, are drawn nowhere in the window; `report.json` and `report.html` keep them.

### 9. The inclusions tree stands in the Folders section

**The sidebar's Folders section holds, under its three fields, `field-vespera.corpus-root`, `field-vespera.working-dir` and `field-vespera.deliverable-root`, the Inclusions tab's parts, in this order**: the tree, `inclusions-tree`; its outcome line, `inclusions-outcome`; Save and Reset, `inclusions-save` and `inclusions-reset`, in one row; and the folders not relevant, `not-relevant-list` with its action (ADR-334 §6). Each does what ADR-345 §2 has it do, and keeps its caption (ADR-430 §8). The sidebar's width, 420 px as it opens and no less than 220 px as the operator drags it (ADR-417 §1), is unchanged.

**No text of the control panel points at an Inclusions tab**:

- `ChildCommand`'s reason for never passing `label <file>` says the window sets the labels in the Folders section, by folder.
- The `foldersNotWalked` row of the Profile window and of the next invocation's values, which the window does not edit there, links *Edit it in its section*, as `seedFolder`'s does, and the link opens the sidebar's Folders section.
- `profile-texts.json`'s short text of `foldersNotWalked` says to choose them in the Folders section.

### 10. The modules, and run ids

**In `ui`**: `Cockpit`, `ProgressCharts`, which keeps `tickText`, `timeTick` and `shortened` as they are, `StepChartFile` in place of `StageChartFile`, `StepsChart`, `EndingPanel`, `ChildCommand`, `ProfileRow`, `profile-texts.json` and `control-panel.css`; `LiveReport` and `InclusionsTab` go, their parts in the classes that draw them now. **In `pipeline`**: `JobSampler` alone, listed in `PipelineHoldsOnlyTheRulesOnRecordTest` as holding no rule. No stage's version names `pipeline` (ADR-222) or the control panel, so **no run id moves**. `report.json`, its `format` and `report.html` are unchanged.

## Alternatives refused

- **A chart for each stage, kept beneath the legs.** Stage 5's chart would draw the steps of two legs, which is what the operator asked to be rid of.
- **A small chart beside each step's bar (the prototype's B), or a strip of leg cards with one leg's bars beside its charts (C).** The operator chose A.
- **The words *not started* on a chart not started.** The operator kept a chart not started wordless: its title and its bar say so.
- **The earlier invocation's samples drawn for a step finished in an earlier invocation.** ADR-418 stopped keeping a chart past the next job, and the database holds no sample.
- **A step finished in an earlier invocation drawn with one sample of its kinds**, as ADR-418 §3 drew a stage's. It is the lone dot the operator asked to be rid of; the step's bar and the report say how far it got.
- ***Finished at* the time this invocation found the step finished, on a step finished in an earlier invocation.** It would say the step finished then.
- **The window telling a step finished in an earlier invocation from its one sample and no `done`.** A step that ran in one reading and said no counter leaves the same file; only the jar knows which steps its runs had recorded finished.
- **A step's key written as the invocation reaches its run**, as ADR-418 §2 writes a stage's. Relevance scoring's run is reached at embedding scoring, so its chart would say *no count reported* before it ran.
- **The stage files deleted.** ADR-382 §4 has nothing delete a chart's file; the window reads none.
- **The column scrolling as a whole.** The legs area would grow with its legs and never scroll on its own, and the output would scroll out of sight with them.
- **Open report kept to scroll to the panel's report parts.** They stand in the panel it is a button of.
- **The status line kept in the header once the invocation has ended.** The ending panel says how it ended, from the same report.

## Consequences

- A bar and its chart stand together: an open leg shows its steps' bars and, beneath them, their charts.
- A step finished in an earlier invocation says so in words, in place of a dot at 0 s; a step that said no counter says it reported no count.
- The output and the ending panel take the column's whole width, and the operator sizes them against the legs.
- The working directory holds up to fifteen small files, of 256 samples a line at most, beside the eight stage files an earlier version left, which nothing reads.
- The cockpit has no tabs: what the Report tab said of a running invocation is in the header, and of an ended one in the ending panel; the folders not walked and not relevant are edited in the Folders section.

## Known limits

- **A step running before its first counter line says *no count reported*** until the reading after the line; the census of fewer than 1,000 entries says it to its end, as ADR-382's second Known limit draws it.
- **Where the runs reading at the invocation's start fails, a step finished in an earlier invocation is drawn as one that ran**, one sample of its kinds and *no count reported*.
- **A short window shrinks the legs area**: the header, the actions and the output's 300 px stand above it whatever the window's height, until the operator drags the divider.
- **The stage files an earlier version wrote stay in the working directory**, read by nothing.

## What this does not decide

- **The least height of either side of the divider**, the split's own.
- **`report.html`'s charts**, ADR-362 §6's, unchanged.
- **The display and what was opened** in the window: `report.json` and `report.html` keep them.
