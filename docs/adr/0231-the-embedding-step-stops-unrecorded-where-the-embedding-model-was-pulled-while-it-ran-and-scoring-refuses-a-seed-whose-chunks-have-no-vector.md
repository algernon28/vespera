## Context

Since ADR-228 a scoring run names the digest and weight dtype Ollama reports for the embedding model when the run is minted, and every step of the run reads the vectors of the one embedder identity under them. `ChunkEmbedder` stores each chunk under what Ollama reports when that chunk is embedded (ADR-084), and is deliberately not handed the run's artefact. The embedding step, `EmbeddingScoringTasklet`, embeds the corpus survivors first and the usable seeds after them, and then records its completion under the run.

**So a pull that lands while the step runs splits the run's chunks between two identities**, and the step records itself finished all the same. What follows depends on which side of the pull a document fell:

- **A survivor embedded after it** has no vector under the run's identity. `RelevanceScoring.scoreAndRecord` throws on the first such survivor. Loud.
- **A seed embedded after it** has none either. `RelevanceScoring.residentSeedVectors` leaves a seed with no vector out of the resident seeds and says nothing. The seeds are embedded last, so a pull late in the step falls among them. Where at least one seed was embedded before the pull, the run scores every survivor against the seeds that are left.

**The second is the defect.** A score is a maximum over the seeds (ADR-020). With a seed missing, every score of the run is computed against a definition of relevance narrower than the one the operator supplied, on the right scale, with no line saying so. It was reachable before ADR-228 too, for a usable seed with no stored vector for any other reason; ADR-228 added a way in.

**Confirmed, not only read.** On the code at `75e28ac5`, by `pipeline.APullWhileTheEmbeddingStepRunsInvocationTest`, whose scripted runtime reports another digest once it has embedded three chunks, the two corpus documents and one of two seeds:

- the invocation **exits 0**; the embedding step is recorded as finished under the run of the first digest; and **both corpus documents are scored** under it, one seed having its vector under each digest. That is the ticket's case, reproduced.
- the next invocation arrives at the run of the new digest, sends every chunk again, stores the vectors missing under that digest, and scores every document. The first run keeps its two scores.
- with the first digest reported again, the invocation arrives at the first run, finds its embedding step recorded, embeds nothing, and **the seed stays without a vector under that run's identity for good**.

And by hand, one seed's vectors moved under another digest's identity and the record of scoring's completion removed: scoring ran again against the one seed left and recorded both scores. That invocation then failed later, at 6a, with `cluster 0 was arranged with no members`. **Read from the code, not measured further**: scored against the one seed left, the documents the moved seed had won have another winning seed; the clustering step, finding survivors of that seed partition with no cluster, forms the clusters again (ADR-230 §2); and 6a, finding its arrangement recorded under the same arrangement run, writes its page from the recorded clusters and asks `LeadDocument.of` for the lead of a cluster whose seed partition no document is in any more. It is a state the test makes, by removing a completion record, and no pull does: no shipped path computes a recorded run's scores again.

## Decision

Both of the ticket's two, each for the case the other does not close.

### 1. The embedding step asks Ollama once more at its end, and stops unrecorded where the answer is not the run's

When `EmbeddingScoringTasklet` has embedded its last seed chunk, and before it records its completion, it asks whether Ollama still reports, for the embedding model, the artefact the scoring run was minted under (ADR-228 §2). Where it does, the step ends as it does today. **Where it does not, the step stops as ADR-202's refusal stops it**: one line at error level, the step failed, no exception thrown, **no completion recorded**, and the job ends there, so nothing is scored in that invocation.

The line names the embedding model and the scoring run, says the model was pulled again while its chunks were being embedded, that the step is not recorded as finished and nothing was scored, and that running again embeds and scores under the model as it is now.

**The question is `StageRuns`'**, which holds both the Ollama client and the artefact it minted the run under: `boolean embeddingModelArtefactStillReported()`. The tasklet reads an answer and acts on it, and names no type it did not name already.

**It is asked once, at the end, and not for every chunk.** `ChunkEmbedder` already asks Ollama for the artefact once for each chunk, to store the vector under it (ADR-228, *What this does not decide*); it is not handed the run's artefact and is not changed. A step that stopped on the first chunk under another digest would stop sooner, and would need `embedding` to be told what the run names, which ADR-228's call 7 refused. The work a later stop wastes is not wasted: the vectors stored after the pull are the ones the next invocation's run reads.

**It is not asked where the step finds its completion recorded.** That invocation embeds nothing, and the run it arrived at is the one Ollama's answer of a moment before named.

### 2. Scoring refuses a usable seed whose chunks have no vector under the run's identity, as it refuses a survivor

`RelevanceScoring.residentSeedVectors` throws an `IllegalStateException` on the first seed handed to it for which `VectorCache.vectorsFor` answers nothing under the embedder identity. The message names the seed occurrence and the identity, and says what it would cost to go on. `RelevanceScoringTasklet` gains no branch for it: the exception leaves its work as `scoreAndRecord`'s does, and no completion is recorded.

**What stood under the run before the step stands after it.** `relevance-scoring` is a tasklet step in one transaction (`TaskletSteps.taskletStep`), and the discard the step begins with is in it, so the exception rolls the discard back with everything else, **as it does on a survivor's refusal**. The first writing of this section said the scores stay discarded; the build's `./mvnw verify` showed two standing. On a shipped path nothing stands to be put back: the scores and the record of the step's completion are written in that one transaction, so a scoring run with scoring unrecorded has no score, and the discard is of nothing. Rows stand under an unrecorded scoring only where a completion record was removed by hand, as the test of this does. **And nothing reads them as a finished scoring**: the job is fifteen steps in a row, a failed step ends it, so the floor's step, clustering, the relevance report, 6a and 6b do not run in the invocation of the refusal, and in the next one scoring is tried again before any of them. `vespera label` reads the run the label file names, which the relevance report wrote in an invocation whose scoring did not fail (ADR-229); what it reads of that run was not traced for this record. The step's wiring is not changed here: it is the survivor's path too.

**An unusable seed is not refused, because it is never asked about.** `RelevanceScoringTasklet.seedContentHashes` takes the unusable seeds of the measurement run out of the seed walk's occurrences before any vector is read, and `EmbeddingScoringTasklet.usableSeedOccurrences` does the same before any is embedded. What `residentSeedVectors` is handed is the seeds extraction produced text from, each of which the embedding step was given to embed. A seed set with unusable seeds in it is scored as it was.

### 2a. A usable seed with no chunk is left out, with a line, and is not refused

**A usable seed can have nothing to embed.** A seed is usable where the text of its conversion, every item of it, holds a letter or a digit (`SeedExtractionItemProcessor`, through `DoclingDocumentTexts.lines`). `HybridChunker` leaves out the items labelled `page_header` and `page_footer`. So a seed whose only text is in page headers and footers is usable and chunks to nothing; the embedding step is given it, embeds no chunk, and stores no vector. **Measured**: `aSeedWithNothingToEmbedIsLeftOutAndScoringGoesOn`, against the first build of this record, records no unusable seed for such a file and fails at scoring on it, in every invocation, with nothing an operator can do but take the file out of the seed folder.

**That is not the fault §2 is for.** A vector gone missing is an accident of the run, and going on hides it. A seed with nothing to embed is a fact about the seed, the same on every run, and is what `CONTEXT.md` says of an unusable seed: it narrows the definition of relevance, is reported, and stops nothing. Before this record it was left out without a word.

**So `RelevanceScoring.residentSeedVectors` is handed, for each usable seed, its content hash and how many chunks it has, leaves out a seed with none and tells its caller which, and refuses a seed with a chunk and no vector (§2).** The two values travel as `embedding.SeedChunks(String contentHash, int chunkCount)`. `RelevanceScoringTasklet.seedContentHashes` looks both up for every usable seed, the count from `HybridChunker.chunkCount`, **with no branch on either**. `embedding` writes no line (ADR-041), so it tells its caller through `ScoringProgress.seedLeftOutWithNoChunk(OccurrenceId)`, and the tasklet, which owns the lines of its stage (ADR-192 §5), writes one at warning level, giving the seed occurrence's id, in every invocation in which scoring does its work. A seed left out is still counted as gone through, so the counter of seeds ends at its total. A seed the pull took has its chunks stored, the step that embeds having chunked it before it embedded, so it is still refused.

**The first amendment put the leave-out in `RelevanceScoringTasklet`, and the architect's gate on PR 498 moved it.** A condition of `pipeline`'s own that decides which seeds every survivor is scored against is a rule in ADR-222's sense: it has the shape of ADR-222's rule 4, which ADR-226 moved to `synthesis.ClusterExemplars.gathered`, and ADR-226 §3 refuted a branch in `pipeline` whose edit moves no run id. As built first, an edit to that condition would have changed every score of the next run and minted nothing.

**The chunk cache says "no chunk" and "never chunked" alike.** A seed the embedding step never reached would be left out with the line, not refused. No shipped path reaches scoring that way: the embedding step chunks every usable seed before it can end, recorded or not, and a step that fails ends the job.

**Scoring's gated line stays, word for word, and is reached in one case fewer.** *"… none has a stored vector under {} …"* still ends the step where no single identity answers for the run, in which case no seed's vectors are read (ADR-228 §4). It no longer ends it where an identity answers and no seed has a vector under it: that is now the first seed refused.

### 3. Why both

**§1 alone leaves the silent case open by every other way in.** It compares two answers, one at the mint and one at the end. A pull and a pull back between them, vectors removed by hand, a database carried over from a build before this one with a run recorded as embedded and a seed short: none is seen. §2 is what makes a missing seed loud whatever left it missing, and is the smaller change.

**§2 alone leaves a run that can never be finished.** With the embedding step recorded as finished under a run one of whose seeds has no vector, every invocation that arrives at that run finds the embedding done, embeds nothing, and throws at scoring. Nothing shipped takes a completion record back, and a statement that did would be a new delete from a table keyed by a run, which `EveryTableKeyedByARunIsOnRecordTest` and ADR-229 exist to make a decision of its own. §1 keeps the record from being written in the first place: the run stays unembedded, and an invocation that arrives at it again embeds every chunk again, storing the ones it lacks.

### 4. Which side of the line a missing seed is on

The operator's standing preference is that a bad row fails the occurrence and not the run, and that a stop of the run is for a fault that poisons every row. A corpus document that cannot be read is the first kind, and stage 2 records it and goes on.

**A seed with no vector is the second kind.** A seed is not a candidate and carries no verdict; it is part of the measure. Every score of the run is a maximum over the seeds, so one seed missing changes what every score means, and which documents fall under a floor read off those scores. There is no occurrence to fail in its place: recording the seed as unusable would say extraction produced no text from it, which is false, and would narrow the definition of relevance by a rule where today it is narrowed by an accident.

**And the stop costs one invocation.** After a pull the run in hand is not arrived at again while Ollama reports the new digest (ADR-228 §1), so nothing scored under it would be read by the next invocation in any case.

### 5. What happens after the stop

| Then | What the invocation does |
| --- | --- |
| Run again, Ollama reporting the new digest | It arrives at the scoring run of the new digest (ADR-228 §1), whose embedding step is not recorded. Every chunk is sent again; the ones embedded after the pull are found stored and no second row is written; the rest are stored. It scores against every usable seed and goes on. The interrupted run keeps its row and no score, and is read by nothing. |
| Run again, Ollama reporting the earlier digest again | It arrives at the interrupted run, whose embedding step is not recorded, and embeds every chunk again under the earlier digest, storing the ones the pull took. It scores against every usable seed. Nothing is stuck. |
| The model is pulled again during that second embedding | The same stop, under the run of that invocation. |
| A pull lands after the last chunk and before the question | Every vector is under the run's identity and the step stops all the same. The next invocation arrives at another run in any case. |
| A pull and a pull back, both while the step runs | §1 sees nothing: the two answers agree. The step is recorded. A survivor or a seed embedded between them has no vector under the run's identity, and scoring throws on it (§2, or `scoreAndRecord`), in that invocation and in every later one that arrives at that run, until a pull or any other change of the run's identity arrives at another. Nothing shipped embeds the missing chunk under the earlier run. **This is the one case left stuck, and it is loud.** A survivor in that position was already stuck before this record. |
| A step of the run that is recorded and done again later (ADR-230's path for clustering) | Not touched. The embedding step and scoring each honour their own completion record and are not done again under the same run; clustering done again reads the vectors of survivors, which scoring has already required. |

### 7. Which run ids move

A stage's implementation version is the last commit touching a module `StageModules` names for it (ADR-058), and a run's id is a hash of its recorded settings and of its upstream runs' ids too (ADR-048).

**The build touches two modules**: `embedding` (`RelevanceScoring`, `ScoringProgress`, the new `SeedChunks`, and javadoc in `EmbedderIdentity`) and `pipeline` (`EmbeddingScoringTasklet`, `StageRuns`, and since the amendment `RelevanceScoringTasklet`). A javadoc edit in `embedding` is a commit touching `embedding`. It touches nothing of `corpus`, `extraction`, `similarity`, `synthesis`, `profile` or `ledger`.

**Read against `StageModules` at `75e28ac5`**, where byte-level reduction names `corpus`; extraction `extraction` and `similarity`; content census and content redundancy `similarity` and `extraction`; seed measurement and embedding scoring `embedding` and `extraction`; arrangement `synthesis`, `extraction` and `embedding`; generation `synthesis`, `extraction`, `embedding` and `profile`:

- **Byte-level reduction, extraction, content census and content redundancy keep their run ids.** None names `embedding`, and no stage names `pipeline`.
- **Seed measurement and embedding scoring are minted again**, by the commit to `embedding`.
- **Arrangement and generation are minted again**, each naming `embedding`, and through their upstream runs.

No recorded setting changes, so `RunIdentityGoldenTest` is not edited.

**In the one build** stages 2 to 6b are minted once already (ADR-228 §7, ADR-225 §8), and this record adds no move to them, **on the condition ADR-228 states**: that no build with one of these changes and without the others is run over a working directory first. Nothing in the repository prevents that.

**What does not move**: no DDL, so no schema version, no table and no index; no cache key; no `run.stage` value, no `finished_step.step` value and no verdict reason. One line of the log is new, the embedding step's stop.

### 9. The calls, every one the session's

12. **The scoring step's transaction is left as it is** (§2), by the coordinating session's decision, passed to the session that wrote this record: taking the discard out of it would change what a survivor's refusal leaves too. The record is corrected to what the wiring does, and the test of it reads what the invocation said it stopped on, where it read a count of scores.
13. **A usable seed with no chunk is left out with a line** (§2a). *Put and not taken*: **refusing it**, which stops a healthy run for good over a file whose only fault is that it is a cover page; **recording it as an unusable seed at seed extraction**, which is the better place for it and is a change to what *usable* means, to `extraction` or to seed extraction's writer, and to what `unusable_seed` and the seed/corpus comparison count, none of which this ticket holds; and **leaving it out without a line**, which is the silence again.
14. **The leave-out is `embedding`'s, and the line reaches the log by a callback**, since the architect's gate (§2a). *Put and not taken*: **`embedding` logging the line itself**, which no class of `embedding` does (ADR-041), `ScoringProgress` being how `RelevanceScoring` already tells its caller what became of a seed; and **a second map of counts beside the map of content hashes**, which lets the two disagree about which seeds there are. The price is one type, `SeedChunks`, and one list of the guard.

## Consequences

- **A pull of the embedding model while the embedding step runs fails that invocation**, with one line saying why, where it succeeded and scored. The next invocation does what it would have done: embeds and scores under the run of the model as it is.
- **No run scores against fewer seeds than have something to embed.** Where a usable seed has a chunk and no vector under the run's identity, scoring throws and names it. A usable seed with no chunk is left out, as it was, and a line now names it.
- **A run whose embedding a pull interrupted is not recorded as embedded**, so it is embedded in full if Ollama ever reports its digest again.
- **A pull and a pull back within one embedding step leaves a run that fails at scoring on every invocation that arrives at it**, until its identity changes. Nothing shipped repairs it. Before this record the same run scored without the seed and said nothing, or, for a survivor, failed in the same way.
- **A second identity under a run's own digest and weight dtype fails every later invocation in which the clustering step has work to do**, until that identity's rows are gone from `vector`, and nothing shipped removes them (§6, point 1). This was so before this record; ADR-228 did not say it.
- **A seed whose only text is page headers and footers is usable, has no vector and wins no document**, and the log says so at warning level on every invocation that scores. No report lists it and no row records it.
- **A refusal at scoring leaves the scores that stood under the run**, by rollback, which on a shipped path is none.
- **Where every usable seed has no chunk**, scoring ends by its gated line, after a warning for each, and the line's count is of the seeds that produced text, as before.
- **A working directory in which an earlier build scored a run against fewer seeds keeps those scores under that run.** No run minted before the build is arrived at again (§7), so nothing reads them.
- **An invocation that embeds asks Ollama one question more**, `/api/tags`, once, at the end of the step.
- **`Stage 5d (relevance scoring, seed vectors read)` ends at its total or not at all.**

## What this does not decide

- **Repairing a run recorded as embedded with a chunk missing under its identity** (§5, a pull and a pull back). It would take back a completion record, or have the embedding step check what it stored where it finds itself recorded. Neither is here.
- **Stopping the embedding step at the first chunk stored under another digest**, sooner than its end.
- **Removing the vectors of a pull nothing reads, or of a second identity under a run's own digest.** Nothing does (ADR-228).
- **Measuring the read of the embedder identities for the pattern of three parts** (§6, point 2).
- **Recording a seed with no chunk as an unusable seed**, at seed extraction, so that the measurement run, the seed/corpus comparison and the reports count it as one (§9, call 13).
- **A corpus survivor whose only text is page headers and footers.** Read from the code and not run: stage 2's no-text floor reads every item (`ExtractedText`), so such a document survives, chunks to nothing, and `scoreAndRecord` throws on it in every invocation, under a message that calls the case impossible by construction (#108). It was so before this record, which leaves the behaviour as it is and has `scoreAndRecord`'s javadoc, which called the case one that cannot happen, corrected in its build (ADR-216 §7). No ticket holds the behaviour: the coordinating session is to raise it with the operator.
- **6a writing its page from a recorded arrangement whose clusters have since been formed again under other seed partitions** (Context). No shipped path computes a recorded run's scores again.
- **Taking the discard of the scoring step out of its transaction** (§9, call 12).
- **Whether the operator should be told, in the invocation after a pull, that the scoring run is new because the model's digest changed.** ADR-228 left it; this record's line is written in the invocation of the pull and not in the one after.
